Culture Stack

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 July 2026

The short version

  • Your library lives in your own iCloud account. We cannot see it.
  • The social features are optional. Nothing is shared until you choose to sign in and follow someone.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no trackers. There is no third-party tracking SDK in this app.
  • We do not sell your data. Not to anyone, for any purpose.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app, and it is actually deleted.

1. Who we are

Culture Stack is published by Persue Apps LLC ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, about your data, or you want something removed, email ncorse@icloud.com. A real person reads it.

2. Your library stays on your devices

Everything you track — albums, games, books, films, shows, your ratings, notes, lists and collection — is stored on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, synced through Apple's CloudKit private database under your own Apple Account.

That is your private iCloud storage. We have no access to it, no ability to read it, and no server that receives it. If you turn iCloud off, your library simply stays local to the device. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. The social features are opt-in

Culture Stack has an optional social layer — a friend feed, reactions, comments, circles and profiles. None of it runs until you sign in. If you never sign in, no account is created and nothing about you leaves your device except the metadata lookups described in section 4.

Signing in

We use Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an anonymous user identifier. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we never see your real email address. We do not request your Apple Account password and never receive it.

What is stored when you use the social features

This data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which acts as our data processor. It is the only backend service that receives personal data from this app.

Your whole library is never uploaded. Only the specific items you publish an activity for are sent. Private notes, unrated items and anything still sitting in your queue stay on your device.

4. Looking up cover art and details

When you add something or ask the app to fill in missing details, Culture Stack sends the title or search term — and nothing about you — to whichever of these public catalogues is relevant:

  • Apple iTunes Search, MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive — music
  • IGDB (via Twitch) and HowLongToBeat — games
  • Open Library — books
  • TMDB — films
  • TVmaze and TheTVDB — television

These requests carry no account identifier, no device identifier and no advertising ID. Each service will, like any web server, see the network request and your IP address, and each has its own privacy policy. We do not send them anything else about you.

5. Device permissions

  • Apple Music — only if you grant it. Used to search the catalogue and play music in the app. Your Apple Music activity is handled by Apple under Apple's policy; we do not receive or store your listening history.
  • Camera — only used to scan a friend's QR code. Frames are processed on device to read the code and are never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • Photos — only the single image you pick as a profile photo. Nothing else in your library is read.
  • Notifications — reminders you schedule. These are generated on your device.

6. What we deliberately do not do

  • No analytics or telemetry SDK. We do not measure your taps, sessions or screens.
  • No advertising, ad networks or ad identifiers.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking. Nothing here feeds an advertising profile.
  • No selling, renting or trading of personal data — there is no circumstance in which we do this.
  • No third-party SDK that profiles you. Firebase is used solely as a database for the social features.

7. Purchases

Culture Stack Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. We never see or store your card number, billing address or Apple Account credentials. We receive only a signed receipt confirming that a valid purchase exists, which is what unlocks Pro features on your devices.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Social data stays until you remove it. You are in control of all of it:

  • Delete a post, comment or reaction — removed immediately.
  • Delete your account — in the app, under your profile. This deletes your profile, friend code, published activities, presence and follow relationships, and signs you out. It is not recoverable.
  • Your library is separate. Deleting your social account does not touch it; it is in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the local copy.
  • Export — you can export your library from within the app at any time.

Copies may persist briefly in routine encrypted backups after deletion, and are overwritten on the normal backup cycle.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR in the UK and EEA, and the CCPA in California — you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, obtain a portable copy, and object to certain processing. The in-app export and delete-account tools give you access, portability and erasure directly. For anything else, email ncorse@icloud.com and we will respond within 30 days.

We have never sold or shared personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA, so there is nothing to opt out of.

10. Children

Culture Stack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our processors — Apple and Google — operate globally, so if you use the social features your data may be processed in the United States. Both operate under standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards for transfers out of the UK and EEA.

12. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Social data is protected by server-side rules that scope each record to its owner — you can only write your own profile, your own activities and your own reactions. Your library is protected by Apple's iCloud encryption. No system is perfect, and we will not claim otherwise; if a breach ever affects your data we will tell you.

13. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app. Continuing to use Culture Stack after a change means you accept the updated policy.

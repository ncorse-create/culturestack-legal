Fastest route

Email ncorse@icloud.com with your device, iOS or macOS version, and what you expected to happen. If something looks wrong on screen, a screenshot saves a round trip.

My library isn't syncing between devices

Culture Stack syncs through your own iCloud account, so the usual causes are on the account side:

  • Signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices?
  • Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Drive switched on?
  • Culture Stack enabled in that same iCloud app list?
  • Enough iCloud storage left?

If all four are fine, open Profile → iCloud in the app and tap Retry Sync. Sync also pauses on cellular when Low Data Mode is on.

Something is missing after I reinstalled

Deleting the app removes the local copy, not the iCloud copy. Reinstall, sign in to the same Apple Account and give the first launch a minute on Wi-Fi — the library refills itself. Nothing is deleted from iCloud unless you delete it in the app.

Managing or cancelling Pro

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. On iPhone or iPad open Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Mac open App Store → your name → Account Settings. Cancelling leaves Pro active until the end of the paid period.

Bought Pro and it isn't showing on a second device? Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Refunds are Apple's to issue, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account or your data

  • Social account — in the app, under your profile. This removes your profile, friend code, published activity, presence and follow relationships. It is not recoverable.
  • Your library — separate, and untouched by the above. It lives in your iCloud; delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove the local copy.
  • Export first if you want a copy — the export lives in the app.

Reporting someone, or something you shouldn't be seeing

Every comment and profile has a report action, and you can block a person outright — blocking severs the follow in both directions and clears their content from your view. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. You can also email ncorse@icloud.com directly; include a screenshot and the display name.

Cover art or details came back wrong

Metadata comes from public catalogues — iTunes, MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, IGDB, Open Library, TheTVDB and TVmaze. Every field is editable: open the item and tap Edit. If a whole catalogue looks broken rather than one entry, tell us which section and we'll look.

Requesting a feature, or reporting a bug

Same address. Feature requests genuinely get read and several shipped ones started as a single email. For a bug, the app can export a diagnostic log — Settings → Diagnostics → Export Diagnostic Logs — which is far more useful than a description.
